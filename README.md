# D2C Skincare E-Commerce Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** developed to analyze the performance of a Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) skincare e-commerce business.

The dashboard provides insights into **sales, profitability, products, customers, geographic performance, sales channels, acquisition channels, and product returns**.

The goal of the project is to transform raw e-commerce data into actionable business insights that can support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

This project focuses on answering key business questions:

* How are overall sales, orders and profit performing?
* Which products and categories generate the most orders?
* Which products are underperforming?
* Which states contribute the highest sales and profit?
* Who are the highest-value customers?
* Which acquisition channels attract the most customers?
* Which sales channels generate the most orders?
* What are the major reasons for product returns?
* How do customer demographics influence sales and orders?

---

## 📌 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Summary

Provides a high-level overview of business performance through key KPIs including:

* Total Revenue
* Total Orders
* Total Profit
* Return Rate
* Total Customers
* Average Order Value
* Profit Margin
* Total Quantity Sold

**Key purpose:** Quickly understand the overall health of the business.

---

### 2. Product Analysis

Analyzes product and category-level performance.

Key analysis includes:

* Total products
* Average delivery days
* Category-wise orders and quantity
* Top 10 products
* Low-performing products
* Median cost price
* Median MRP
* Skin-type distribution
* Year-wise product order trends

**Key purpose:** Identify high-performing products and opportunities for product-level optimization.

---

### 3. State Wise Analysis

Analyzes geographic sales and profitability.

Key analysis includes:

* State-wise sales
* State-wise profit
* State-wise order growth
* State-wise order distribution
* Customer distribution by gender
* City-wise sales performance
* Skin-concern analysis

**Key purpose:** Identify high-performing markets and geographic opportunities.

---

### 4. Customer Analysis

Provides insights into customer behavior and customer value.

Key analysis includes:

* Total customers
* Customer ratings
* Sales revenue by acquisition channel and gender
* Customer count by acquisition channel
* Top 10 customers by sales revenue
* Bottom 10 customers by sales revenue
* Sales revenue by age group
* Orders by age group
* Customers associated with returned products

**Key purpose:** Understand customer segments and identify high-value customers.

---

### 5. Sales Channel Performance

Analyzes sales and acquisition channel performance.

Key analysis includes:

* Customer acquisition by year and channel
* Orders by year and acquisition channel
* Return reasons
* Top returned products
* Yearly sales trends
* Orders by sales channel
* Orders by acquisition channel

**Key purpose:** Evaluate channel performance and understand return patterns.

## ⚙️ Technical Implementation

* Cleaned and transformed the dataset using Power Query.
* Built relationships between multiple data tables.
* Developed DAX measures for business KPIs.
* Created calculated metrics for profitability and return analysis.
* Designed an interactive multi-page Power BI dashboard.
* Used slicers and interactive visualizations for data exploration.
* Analyzed sales performance across products, customers, states and channels.
* Created geographic visualizations for city/state-level analysis.

---

## 📈 Key KPIs

The dashboard tracks important business metrics such as:

| KPI                 | Purpose                                    |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Total Revenue       | Measures overall sales generated           |
| Total Orders        | Measures order volume                      |
| Total Profit        | Measures business profitability            |
| Profit Margin %     | Measures profitability relative to revenue |
| Return Rate %       | Measures returned orders                   |
| Total Customers     | Measures customer base                     |
| Average Order Value | Measures average revenue per order         |
| Total Quantity      | Measures units sold                        |
